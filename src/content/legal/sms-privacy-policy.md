title: "SMS Privacy Policy"
description: "How Fused Technologies handles your information when you opt in to SMS messages."
---

Fused Technologies respects your privacy. By opting into our SMS messaging service, you agree to the following terms regarding how we handle your data:

## Data Collection

We collect your name and mobile phone number when you sign up for SMS updates.

## Data Usage

We use your data solely for appointment reminders, account verification and providing technical support.

## Data Security

We protect your data through encryption and secure storage measures to prevent unauthorized access.

## Data Retention

We retain your information as long as you are subscribed to our SMS service. You may request deletion at any time.

## Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe from our SMS list. After unsubscribing, we will remove your number from our list within 24 hours.

## Non-Sharing Clause

Your information is only shared with our SMS service provider to enable messaging.
